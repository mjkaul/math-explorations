# math-explorations

I worked with Claude to make this little site designed to help my daughter "get" math—not necessarily to understand how to get more answer right, but rather to understand why math is beautiful and fascinating. It's math for people who think they don't like math because they like philosophy and history and other supposedly more creative pursuits. I wish schools could do more to cultivate this big-picture vision of math. In the meantime, let me know if you have ideas for other activities to add!

View the site [here](https://mjkaul.github.io/math-explorations/).
