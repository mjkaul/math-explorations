# math-explorations

I worked with Claude to make this little site designed to help my daughter "get" math—not to get more answers right, but rather to see why math is beautiful and fascinating. Math for people who think they don't like math, who prefer the humanities, or who simply don't like how math is currently taught. I wish schools could do more to cultivate this big-picture vision of math. In the meantime, let me know if you have ideas for other activities to add!

View the site [here](https://mjkaul.github.io/math-explorations/).
